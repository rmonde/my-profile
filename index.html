I started out as a Unix L1 engineer in 2009 and grew into a Solution Architect & Technical Lead at Globant, where I lead cloud and DevOps engagements for enterprise customers. My day-to-day blends hands-on platform engineering with pre-sales architecture, talent strategy, and now Generative AI integration.
